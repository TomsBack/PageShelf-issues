# PageShelf Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

PageShelf keeps your reading list on your device. It has no account, no server and no analytics.

## What PageShelf stores

On your device only, in the browser's extension storage (IndexedDB and `storage.local`):
- The pages you save: address, title, site, author, excerpt, tags, reading time, read or unread, favorite and how far you read.
- The offline copy of each page and a small thumbnail of its lead image.
- Your settings, and whether you closed the support banner.

Nothing is sent to the developer or to anyone else. Uninstalling PageShelf deletes all of it. "Delete everything" in settings deletes your saved pages at any time.

## What PageShelf downloads

PageShelf only connects to the websites you save:
- **Saving the open page** reads the page you are on. It may download that page's lead image for the thumbnail.
- **Saving a link or importing a list** downloads each page once, to make its offline copy. This needs access to websites, which PageShelf asks for the first time. The request includes your cookies for that site, the same as opening the link, so pages you can read come through.
- **Reading a saved page** loads its images from the original site, and the reading list may load thumbnails from it. Turn off "Show images from the web" in settings to stop this.

PageShelf never runs scripts from saved pages and never loads their embedded videos or frames.

## Import and export

Files you import are read on your device. Exports are saved to your computer. Neither is uploaded anywhere.

## Permissions

- **Active tab and scripting:** read the page you save, when you save it.
- **Context menus:** the "Save page" and "Save link" menu items.
- **Alarms:** retry downloads later.
- **Storage and unlimited storage:** keep your list and offline copies on your device.
- **Offscreen (Chrome):** turn downloaded pages into clean text.
- **Access to websites (optional):** download pages you save as links or import. Asked for when first needed. Remove it in settings at any time.
